# Design Context — E-COMEX

## Target Audience
Argentine businesses that import goods — importers, commercial companies, purchasing teams. B2B decision-makers who deal with customs bureaucracy, cost uncertainty, and complex regulations daily. They need tools that feel trustworthy and serious, not flashy or consumer-oriented.

## Use Cases
- Evaluate whether a product can be imported before committing
- Understand the real landed cost (FOB + freight + taxes + fees)
- Identify regulatory requirements and interventions
- Generate professional PDF reports for internal review
- Operators create budgets from Excel files

## Brand Personality / Tone
**Institutional, precise, confident.** Like a serious financial tool — not a startup, not a SaaS dashboard. E-COMEX was born from 20+ years of hands-on foreign trade experience. The platform automates what specialists used to do manually. The tone is professional expertise backed by technology, not technology pretending to be expertise.

## Color Palette
- Blue (#2b59ff) — primary, institutional trust
- Violet (#7c3aed) — secondary accent
- Celeste (#38bdf8) — tertiary, system states
- Violaceous (#4c1d95) — depth
- White — text, CTAs
- Gold (#d4a843) — financial values only (totals, costs)

## Typography
- Manrope — headlines, display (extrabold, tight tracking)
- Inter — body, labels, UI text

## Quality Bar
Flagship. This must feel proprietary and impossible to confuse with a generic template or AI output.
